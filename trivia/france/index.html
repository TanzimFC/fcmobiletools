How it works
Select an answer to test yourself, or use Show Answer when you would rather study. Easy days have 3 questions, Medium 4, and Difficult 5. Progress is saved on this device as you complete each day.

About France Nation's Story trivia

France's A Nation's Story chapter covers the national team's football history, from the 1998 World Cup win at home through the Euro 2000 title, the 2006 and 2018 World Cup finals, and the 2022 final in Qatar. Questions move from Easy to Difficult across the nine days, and the difficulty level sets how many correct answers you need to move on.

Every question includes a hint. If you get one wrong, or use Show Answer instead of guessing, you still get the correct answer and a short explanation, so the page works as a straightforward answer guide too.

Frequently asked questions

How many days does France trivia have?

France has 9 days of trivia, split into Easy, Medium and Difficult levels with 3 days each.

How many questions are in each day?

Easy days have 3 questions, Medium days have 4 questions and Difficult days have 5 questions, for 36 questions total.
