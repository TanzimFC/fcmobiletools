# Operating Rhythm and Reports

Use existing GitHub issues, pull requests and free documents. Avoid introducing another platform just to manage a small volunteer team. Keep private personnel or incident records out of public repository issues.

## Cadence

### Weekly: team update
Each active contributor posts a short update by the agreed weekly deadline. A report should take about five minutes.

```text
WEEKLY UPDATE
Name / function:
Week ending:
1. Completed (link evidence):
2. In progress:
3. Next deliverable and due date:
4. Blockers / help needed:
5. Risk or user issue:
6. Estimated time available next week:
```

Leads consolidate updates into one department summary. No response is not automatically misconduct; check whether the person is still available and adjust scope.

### Weekly: leadership review
The founder and leads review:
- The three most important outcomes for the next week.
- Critical bugs, expired codes and urgent content corrections.
- Tasks blocked for more than seven days.
- Workload and volunteer availability.
- Pending decisions, with one named decision owner.
- Security, privacy or moderation incidents.

Keep the meeting short. Record decisions, owners and due dates.

### Monthly: company report
The Operations lead prepares a factual report covering:

1. **Executive status:** what shipped, what failed, what needs a decision.
2. **Product reliability:** key defects, uptime or deployment evidence if available, performance issues and release status.
3. **Content quality:** pages updated, corrections, source quality and expired information.
4. **Growth:** analytics period, sessions/users, search performance, returning users, campaign results and sign-ups where tracking is reliable.
5. **Community:** feedback themes, moderation actions and unresolved complaints.
6. **Financial position:** actual cash received, actual cash spent, commitments and available balance.
7. **People:** active contributors, onboarding, workload and access changes.
8. **Risk and audit:** open findings, severity, owner and due date.
9. **Next month:** no more than five priorities, each with a measurable completion condition.
10. **Founder decisions required:** decision, deadline and consequence of delay.

Always state the reporting period, source and definition for each metric. Label estimates. Do not invent numbers or compare different date ranges as if they were equivalent.

## Task standard

Every meaningful task should have:
- A clear outcome and acceptance criteria.
- One directly responsible person.
- A reviewer when quality or risk requires it.
- A due date agreed with the contributor.
- Links to source, evidence or pull request.
- Status: Backlog, Ready, In progress, Blocked, In review, Done.

"Done" means the acceptance criteria passed and evidence is linked. For a code change, this normally means review and relevant tests passed. For an article, it means factual review and publication checks passed.

## Incident report

For security, privacy, account compromise, broken critical tools or misleading public information, record:
- Time first noticed and affected service.
- Observable facts and evidence, without exposing secrets.
- User impact and severity.
- Immediate containment.
- Person coordinating the response.
- Recovery and verification.
- Whether users or a provider need to be notified.
- Follow-up action and owner.

Escalate suspected credential exposure or user-data exposure immediately to Tanzim. Do not post secrets or private user data in public issues. Preserve evidence and avoid speculative public claims.

## Metrics glossary

Agree on definitions before using a number as a target:
- **Users:** specify active users or unique users and the analytics source.
- **Sessions:** visits counted by the selected analytics system.
- **Search clicks/impressions:** use the same Google Search Console date range.
- **Conversion:** define the exact action and denominator.
- **Reliability:** report measured incidents or verified monitoring, not an unsupported uptime estimate.
- **Revenue:** cash actually received, separated from pending balances and estimates.
- **Spend:** actual payments and committed liabilities, not the value of donated time.

## Report quality check

Before sharing a report, verify the dates, source links, totals, unresolved risks, owner names and due dates. Mark unavailable data as "not measured" rather than guessing.
