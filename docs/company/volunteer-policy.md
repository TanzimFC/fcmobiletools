# Volunteer Policy and Onboarding

## What participation means

FCMOBILETOOLS currently has a $0 operating budget. Roles described as volunteer roles are unpaid contributions unless a separate written agreement says otherwise. Do not describe volunteers as salaried employees or promise future pay, equity, prizes or jobs.

Before recruiting, explain:
- The work and expected deliverable.
- That participation is voluntary and unpaid.
- A realistic weekly time range, with no pressure to exceed it.
- Who reviews the work and how feedback is given.
- What access the role needs.
- How credit and portfolio attribution work.
- How to pause or leave the role.

Do not use unpaid volunteers to make misleading employment claims or imply a guaranteed future reward. Check applicable local law before formalising any ongoing arrangement.

## Recruitment flow

1. Publish a clear role description and required skills.
2. Ask for relevant examples, availability and motivation.
3. Use a small, bounded trial task. Do not request substantial unpaid production work as an audition.
4. Review quality, communication, judgement and ability to follow instructions.
5. Confirm scope, reporting line, permissions and expectations in writing.
6. Add the person to the role register and give only necessary access.
7. Review fit after two to four weeks.

## Role register

Maintain a private register with: contributor name/handle, role, lead, start date, agreed availability, status, granted access, last review, and offboarding date if applicable. Do not store identity documents or sensitive personal information unless there is a lawful and necessary reason.

## Conduct

All contributors must:
- Be respectful and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats and retaliation.
- Protect private information, credentials and unreleased work.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and relevant sponsorship relationships.
- Cite sources and distinguish confirmed facts from speculation.
- Avoid spam, fake reviews, deceptive promotion and unauthorised giveaways.
- Report suspected security incidents promptly.
- Respect licenses, attribution and third-party intellectual property.
- Keep disagreements focused on the work.

## Work ownership and attribution

Before work is merged or published, agree on the intended license, attribution and permissions for code, writing, graphics and other assets. Do not assume that unpaid work automatically transfers all rights to the project. Use a written contribution agreement reviewed for applicable law before accepting material contributions where ownership or licensing is unclear. Never accept copied or unlicensed assets.

## Access rules

- Use individual accounts; never share passwords.
- Enable multi-factor authentication wherever available.
- Give the minimum permission needed for the task.
- Keep production secrets out of source code, issues and screenshots.
- Do not give new volunteers admin access by default.
- Review access monthly and remove it promptly when the role ends.
- Only the owner or specifically authorised maintainer can approve production secrets or privileged account access.

## Offboarding checklist

- [ ] Confirm handover and unfinished tasks.
- [ ] Transfer ownership of project files and documentation where agreed.
- [ ] Remove repository, hosting, analytics and community permissions that are no longer needed.
- [ ] Rotate any shared credential the contributor could access.
- [ ] Record the end date and close open assignments.
- [ ] Thank the contributor and agree on public attribution if appropriate.

Leaving should not result in harassment or retaliation. Do not withhold a promised payment or reward; resolve any separate written commitments fairly.
