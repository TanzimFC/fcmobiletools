After Numero 7 and Numero 4, FC Mobile moves into its third chapter with **Numero 10**.

The No. 10 is traditionally associated with the team's creative player, someone who can control attacks, create chances and change a match with technique and vision.

And Week 3 certainly follows that theme, with **Wayne Rooney** as the main chapter reward and major names such as **Zidane, Hazard, Bergkamp, Dembélé and Pelé** appearing throughout the chapter.

## When Does Numero 10 Start?

Numero 10 officially unlocks on **September 10, 2026**.

It is the third of the four Numero chapters:

- Week 1: **Numero 7**
- Week 2: **Numero 4**
- Week 3: **Numero 10**
- Week 4: **Numero 8**

So Week 3 is also important for players working toward the final **Choose Your Numero** reward because another Numero Kit becomes available.

## The Main Reward: 117 OVR Wayne Rooney

The biggest reward in the Numero 10 Puzzle Rewards is:

**117 OVR ST Wayne Rooney**

Rooney costs **700 Numero Tokens**.

The rest of the reward path includes:

| Reward | Cost |
| :--- | ---: |
| 6,000,000 Coins + 1,500 Gems | 400 Tokens |
| 400 Star Shards | 200 Tokens |
| 500 Rank Up Points | 500 Tokens |
| FC Draft Voucher | 250 Tokens |
| FC Draft Voucher | 250 Tokens |
| 600 Star Shards | 400 Tokens |
| **117 OVR ST Rooney** | **700 Tokens** |

The basic reward structure is similar to the previous Numero chapters, but the featured player changes to Rooney.

## Daily Training & 10 Changer

Week 3 introduces two main ways to earn Numero Tokens.

### Daily Training

Players can complete:

- 2 Skill Games
- 2 Matches

each day.

There are **7 Daily Training rounds** available during the chapter.

### 10 Changer

The second activity is **10 Changer**.

Complete **7 matches** to earn additional Numero Tokens.

The name itself fits the theme: the No. 10 is traditionally the player responsible for creating and changing attacking situations.

## From 1 to 10 Competition

Week 3 also introduces a special section called **From 1 to 10**.

This is particularly interesting because it provides a large amount of **Gems**.

The quests are divided across two days.

### Day 1

Players can complete objectives such as:

- Log in once
- Win 2 Division Rivals matches
- Draft 3 times
- Assist 4 goals
- Complete 5 matches

Completing the Day 1 milestone gives:

**4,500 Gems**

### Day 2

The requirements increase:

- Draft 6 times
- Assist 7 goals
- Complete 8 matches
- Score 9 goals
- Win 10 Division Rivals matches

The Day 2 milestone gives another:

**4,500 Gems**

That means the two milestones alone provide **9,000 Gems**, with additional 100-Gem rewards attached to the individual objectives.

However, Pelé is a **Box-exclusive** player, so don't confuse him with the players available through the Numero Drafts.

## Why Week 3 Matters

Week 3 is arguably the most attractive chapter so far if you're looking for **attacking players**.

You have:

- **117 OVR Rooney** from the main reward path
- **122 OVR Pelé** in the Box
- **122 OVR Zidane** in the Draft pool
- **122 OVR Dembélé** in the Draft pool
- **121 OVR Hazard**
- **121 OVR Bergkamp**
- **120 OVR Musiala**

Compared with Week 2's heavily defensive lineup, Numero 10 is almost the complete opposite.

## Don't Forget the Bigger Numero Reward

The weekly player isn't the only reason to complete the chapter.

The Numero event is designed around collecting the different **Numero Kits** from each chapter.

After completing the four chapters, players can access the larger **Choose Your Numero** reward.

That means skipping Week 3 completely could hurt you later even if you don't particularly want Rooney.

## Final Verdict

**Numero 10 is probably the strongest chapter so far for attacking players.**

The biggest attractions are **Pelé, Zidane and Dembélé**, while Rooney provides a straightforward 117 OVR reward through the main chapter.

But the smartest approach is not to blindly chase every high-OVR card.

If you're free-to-play, prioritize the **free Tokens, Gems and Draft Vouchers**, complete the daily activities and make sure you progress toward the Numero Kit.

The biggest mistake would be focusing entirely on one shiny 122 OVR card and ignoring the rewards that are guaranteed.

**Week 3 is about the No. 10, but the real objective is still the four-week Numero progression.**
