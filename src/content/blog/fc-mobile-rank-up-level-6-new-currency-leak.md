A new FC Mobile leak has started a very familiar conversation: **are we getting another Rank Up level?**

According to information shared by **Madridistaa on X**, EA may be preparing **Rank Up Level 6**, with a separate currency needed for the new upgrade.

That is the part worth paying attention to.

Not because Rank 6 is confirmed. It is not. It is also **not in the live game right now**.

The interesting part is what a sixth rank would do to the economy we have today, especially after players have suddenly started receiving much larger amounts of Rank Up Points than they used to.

## Rank Up Level 6 is not live yet

Let's get this out of the way first.

**There is no Rank Up Level 6 in the live game at the time of writing.** This is a leak, not an EA announcement.

The current Rank Up system goes through five ranks. EA's documented rank colours run from Green at Rank 1 through Orange at Rank 5. citeturn322904search2

So if this leak turns out to be real, EA would be adding another progression step on top of the system we already have.

And that immediately raises the obvious question:

**What comes after the current Orange/Gold tier? Diamond?**

Maybe. But we do not know the name yet.

I would not add Rank 6 to the calculator's live cost table until EA actually puts it in the game or the information becomes solid enough to verify.

## Why the timing is what makes this interesting

At first glance, giving players another Rank Up level sounds simple. More OVR, another Skill Point, another reason to upgrade a card.

But look at what has been happening with Rank Up Points lately.

Players have been seeing reward amounts that would have looked ridiculous a few months ago. **777 Rank Up Points** has appeared in event rewards. We have also seen **333**, **1,333**, and even **2,000 Rank Up Point** amounts showing up across the current game economy.

That changed the way people looked at EA's reward structure.

For a while, some players were thinking, "EA is finally being generous with Rank Up Points."

Then a leak about a new rank appears.

That makes those rewards look a little different.

Maybe EA was never simply making Rank Up easier. Maybe the economy was being pushed forward because another upgrade tier was already being prepared behind the scenes.

That is speculation, of course. There is no proof that EA increased Rank Up Point payouts specifically because Rank 6 was planned.

But the timing is hard to ignore.

## The hoarding question

This is where I think players need to be careful.

If Rank 6 does use a **new currency**, the current Rank Up Points could become much easier to obtain or much less important for future upgrades.

That would create a strange situation.

Players who spent everything during the current progression period could end up wishing they had kept some resources. Players who hoarded might suddenly have a very comfortable stockpile for Rank 1 to 5 upgrades while the new currency becomes the next bottleneck.

It could even create what I would call a **hoard reset**.

Not necessarily a full season reset. FC Mobile 27 already launched without a traditional season reset, according to EA's own announcements. citeturn239263search0

Instead, EA could reset the economy without resetting the club.

Your players stay.

Your squad stays.

Your account stays.

But the resource you need to keep pushing your best players changes.

That is a much more interesting possibility than simply adding another button called Rank 6.

## Could Rank 6 give another Skill Point?

One community theory is that another Rank Up level could grant another **Skill Point**.

That would make sense with the way the Rank Up system has worked. Rank Ups increase OVR and provide Skill Points as part of player progression. citeturn322904search2

The bigger theory is even more interesting:

**Could Rank 6 unlock a third PlayStyle slot?**

Again, that is only a theory right now.

But if EA combines another Rank Up with another Skill Point and an additional PlayStyle slot, the value of Rank 6 would be much bigger than just **+1 OVR**.

Suddenly a highly ranked card would have another reason to stay relevant.

And that is where the P2W concern comes in.

## Is this another way to sell progression?

This is the part where EA deserves some accountability.

FC Mobile already has an economy where resources are constantly being introduced, changed, limited and repackaged. When a new progression currency arrives, the people who can spend heavily usually have the easiest way to get around the new bottleneck.

F2P players do not have that luxury.

They have to grind events, complete objectives, make smart exchanges and protect every valuable resource. P2W players can simply buy their way further into the new progression when the opportunity appears.

So if Rank 6 arrives with a new currency, the important question should not just be **"How much does Rank 6 cost?"**

It should be:

**How obtainable is that currency for a normal F2P player?**

Can you earn enough through regular events?

Is there a reasonable weekly supply?

Does the Market help at all?

Will the new currency appear in paid packs at a much better rate than free rewards?

And how quickly will the cost climb as OVR keeps going up?

Those are the questions that matter.

Because adding another resource sink every time the OVR ceiling moves can easily turn progression into a treadmill.

## Remember Dudek and Mascherano

There is another reason the Rank Up Point economy feels suspicious to some players.

Older FC Mobile rank-up systems had their own valuable items, with **Dudek and Mascherano** becoming major parts of the progression conversation.

Then the current currency-based system changed the whole approach.

The lesson from that period is simple: **do not assume today's valuable resource will stay equally valuable forever.**

That is why the current flood of Rank Up Points is worth watching.

Maybe EA genuinely wants Rank Ups to be more accessible.

Or maybe the currency is becoming easier to distribute because the next rank will eventually move players onto something new.

The second possibility would explain why amounts such as 777 or 2,000 no longer look completely absurd.

It does not prove anything.

But it is exactly the kind of thing players should question instead of blindly calling EA generous.

## And what happens to the current Rank Up currency?

This may be the biggest practical question.

Suppose Rank 1 through Rank 5 continue using today's Rank Up Points, while Rank 6 requires a new currency.

In that case, today's currency could become much easier to earn because it would only cover the existing five ranks.

That would actually be good for newer players.

A player joining the game later could potentially rank cards without struggling as much to build a huge RP balance.

But there is another side.

If the new currency is scarce, Rank 6 becomes the new bottleneck.

So we would go from:

**"I need more Rank Up Points."**

to:

**"I have thousands of Rank Up Points, but I cannot get the new currency."**

That is not really an easier progression system. It is a new wall.

And if that new wall can be bypassed through paid offers, then we have a pretty obvious reason to be skeptical about the design.

## What about the next reset?

There is also a timing problem.

FC Mobile 27 was launched with **no traditional season reset**, so the current club progression continues into the new season. citeturn239263search0

That makes a future Rank 6 especially interesting.

Why introduce another permanent progression layer if another major reset could eventually wipe or restructure the system anyway?

Maybe EA plans to keep this progression for a long time.

Maybe Rank 6 is designed specifically for the longer FC Mobile 27 cycle.

Or maybe EA is gradually raising the ceiling before the next major change.

We simply do not know yet.

That is why I would not sell the leak as some huge confirmed feature.

## What should you do with your Rank Up Points?

For now, I would not panic.

I also would not burn every last Rank Up Point just because bigger rewards have started appearing.

Keep enough flexibility to upgrade the cards you actually use. Do not dump resources into a card just because it has a high OVR. And do not start buying expensive Market cards based on the assumption that Rank 6 is guaranteed.

For F2P players especially, **having liquid resources is power**.

The same rule applies to the new currency if it ever appears: do not spend it immediately just because EA puts a shiny new Rank 6 button in front of you.

Wait until we know the costs.

Wait until we know the supply.

Then do the math.

That is exactly why the Rank Up Calculator exists.

## The bottom line

**Rank Up Level 6 could be coming, but it is not in the game yet.**

The reported separate currency is the detail I am watching most closely.

A sixth rank could be harmless. It could make progression easier for everyone. It could bring another Skill Point or even another PlayStyle slot.

Or it could become another expensive progression wall that hits F2P players hardest while giving P2W players another thing to spend on.

And honestly, the recent Rank Up Point rewards make the timing interesting.

Maybe EA was finally giving players better rewards.

Maybe they were preparing the economy for what comes next.

We will find out.

For now, **hoard smart, do not panic-spend, and do not treat Rank 6 as confirmed until it actually lands.**
