There is a new card category in EA SPORTS FC 27 that has a very different idea behind it.

It is called **Hall of FUT**.

And now it is showing up in the FC Mobile leak cycle.

That is what makes the current **Unbreakable** leak interesting. MadridistaaFC reported **Theo Walcott, Antonio Valencia and Alessandro Florenzi** in the Unbreakable Draft pools, alongside **Javier Zanetti and Ruud van Nistelrooy** as the 122 OVR headline cards. citeturn212671search3

The obvious question came straight away:

**How can these players suddenly be part of the "legend" conversation when they are not Icons or Heroes?**

The answer starts on console and PC.

## What is Hall of FUT?

EA introduced **Hall of FUT as a new Player Item type for EA SPORTS FC 27**.

The idea is not simply "another Icon."

EA wanted to recognize players who became famous **inside Ultimate Team itself**. Their real-world career might not fit the traditional Icon profile, but their FUT cards became part of the game's culture.

That is why the term **FUT Fame** is useful here.

Think about the players older FUT players remember because of one ridiculous trait. Maybe it was pace. Maybe it was strength. Maybe a weird body type, five-star skills, a perfect link or a card that somehow stayed in squads for months.

EA's first Hall of FUT class was:

- **Adebayo Akinfenwa**
- **Marouane Fellaini**
- **Loïc Rémy**
- **Eljero Elia**
- **Giovani dos Santos**

EA's own announcement says these players may not be Icons, but they created memories that became part of FUT culture. citeturn186672search0

That is the whole point.

## Icons, Heroes and Hall of FUT are not the same thing

The easiest way to understand the three categories is to look at **why EA chose them**.

### Icons

Icons are built around **real football legacy**.

These are the players associated with sustained world-class careers and major historical achievements. EA's FC 27 launch notes describe Icons around trophy cabinets, world-class excellence and players who helped define football history. citeturn926763search1

Pelé, Zidane, Ronaldo Nazário and other all-time greats fit that idea.

### Heroes

Heroes are also rooted in real football, but the story is usually more specific.

A memorable club spell, an unforgettable season, a legendary league run or a player who became a fan favourite in a particular competition can put someone into the Hero conversation. EA describes Heroes around unforgettable moments, celebrated club or league spells and lasting fan impact. citeturn926763search1

### Hall of FUT

Hall of FUT uses a different test:

**What did this player's card mean to FUT players?**

That is why a player can be huge inside the game without having the real-world résumé that would normally make him an Icon.

EA describes Hall of FUT as a new tier celebrating players whose impact in EA SPORTS FC and FUT made them unforgettable through things such as **perfect links, standout performances or memories created in squads**. citeturn926763search1

So Hall of FUT is not "cheap Icons."

It is a different story.

## The cult-card idea is the whole reason Hall of FUT exists

The first class makes much more sense when you stop judging them by football trophies and start thinking like an old FUT player.

**Akinfenwa** became famous for ridiculous physical strength.

**Rémy and Elia** were remembered for pace and running in behind.

**Giovani dos Santos** was one of those dribbling cards players kept finding uses for.

**Fellaini** became the classic physical midfield and aerial threat.

EA's own Hall of FUT write-up leans heavily into these old card identities and memories. citeturn186672search0

That is important because the category is deliberately about **the player item history**, not a second version of the Ballon d'Or.

## Hall of FUT has already expanded beyond the first five

The original July announcement showed the first five inductees, but the category did not stop there.

By September, the FC 27 database had expanded to a much wider group, including names such as:

- **Theo Walcott**
- **Antonio Valencia**
- **Hulk**
- **Alexandre Pato**
- **David Luiz**
- **Mario Balotelli**
- **Miguel Layún**
- **Victor Ibarbo**
- **Loïc Rémy**

Current FC 27 Hall of FUT tracking lists the category as a separate historical Player Item type, with more players added beyond the original class. citeturn212671search0

That makes the Unbreakable leak much easier to understand.

Walcott appearing there is not EA secretly promoting him to Icon status.

He is a **Hall of FUT player** receiving a special event card.

## How Hall of FUT works on console/PC

This is another part that matters when we try to predict FC Mobile.

EA tied the new category directly to the **FUT Gallery**.

At launch, **Base Hall of FUT Player Items** were made available through the **Gallery Token Store**. Players earn Gallery Tokens by completing and grading Gallery Sets, then spend those Tokens in the Token Store. EA also said the Token Store would change during the season. citeturn926763search1turn926763search5

There is another layer too.

EA planned **special versions** of Hall of FUT players through Campaigns during the year, separate from the Base items. citeturn926763search0

So on console the structure is basically:

**Hall of FUT base item → Gallery / Token Store**

**Hall of FUT special version → future Campaign content**

That is a much bigger system than simply adding a new logo to a card.

## And yes, the base cards are not meant to compete with Icons immediately

This is another detail that gets lost when people hear "new legend category."

The first FC 27 Hall of FUT base cards were not released as giant end-game cards. The initial group was around **84-85 OVR**, with their value coming from the card's identity, stats and PlayStyle rather than raw OVR. citeturn212671search8turn186672search3

That fits the idea perfectly.

EA was not saying:

"Here is a new way to make 21 more 90+ Icons."

They were saying:

"These players mattered to FUT, so they deserve their own historical Player Item category."

Special versions can then push those same names into stronger event content later. EA has explicitly separated the Base items from future Campaign versions. citeturn926763search0turn926763search1

## So how could this come to FC Mobile?

This is where things become interesting.

**Hall of FUT is confirmed on EA SPORTS FC 27 console/PC.**

**Hall of FUT is not officially confirmed for FC Mobile yet.**

But the FC Mobile leak is strong enough to make the possibility worth taking seriously.

MadridistaaFC's Unbreakable leak showed:

### Unbreakable Draft B

| **Antonio Valencia** | **121** | Hall of FUT |

The leak itself does not prove the final mobile implementation, but it gives us a very strong clue.

EA may be bringing the **Hall of FUT identity into FC Mobile as a player category**, then giving those players special high-OVR versions through live events.

That would explain why Walcott, Valencia and Florenzi can appear in the same Draft as Zanetti and Van Nistelrooy without turning them into Icons.

## Could Hall of FUT work like an event category instead?

Yes. And this is where we need to separate what is known from what we are predicting.

There are at least **three realistic ways** EA could handle Hall of FUT on mobile.

### Option 1: Hall of FUT becomes a permanent player category

This would be the cleanest implementation.

A player keeps his Hall of FUT identity across the FC Mobile database, just as a player can be tagged as an Icon or Hero.

Then an event can give him a special 120-122 OVR version.

Under this model:

**Walcott = Hall of FUT**

**Unbreakable Walcott = the Unbreakable event version of that Hall of FUT player**

That is the explanation that best fits the current leak.

### Option 2: Hall of FUT becomes a special event pool

EA could also keep the category tightly tied to certain campaigns.

In that case, Hall of FUT would work more like an event identity that appears when the relevant content is active.

This is less clean from a database point of view, but FC Mobile already uses lots of event-specific card identities, so it is possible.

### Option 3: Hall of FUT becomes its own recurring "Heroes-style" mobile program

This is probably the most interesting possibility.

FC Mobile has a long history of using **Icons and Heroes as distinct content pillars**. When FC Mobile launched in 2023, EA was already using Icon and Hero items in early content. citeturn830521reddit20turn830521search0

Later events continued building around those historical player categories.

So EA could decide that Hall of FUT deserves its **own mobile event series**, just as Icons and Heroes have had dedicated journeys, chapters or themed releases.

Imagine a dedicated Hall of FUT campaign where the game rotates through old FUT cult favourites:

**Walcott. Ibarbo. Rémy. Fellaini. Pato. Valencia.**

That would actually fit the concept very well.

## Could it be like the old "Heroes" model on FC Mobile?

This is the comparison I would make, but with one warning.

**The role could be similar. The reason for the category is different.**

Early FC Mobile made Icons and Heroes very visible parts of the new post-FIFA-Mobile era. The launch period and early events used them as recognizable historical-player rewards. citeturn830521reddit20turn830521search0

Hall of FUT could end up filling a similar **content role** for FC Mobile:

- a recognizable group of historical/cult players
- their own visual identity
- recurring special cards
- event chapters built around them
- high-OVR versions later in the cycle

But Hall of FUT would still be about **FUT Fame**, not simply real-world football fame.

That distinction is the whole reason EA created it.

## Why the Unbreakable leak is such a big clue

Look at the six reported players again.

You have:

**Icon**
- Javier Zanetti
- Ruud van Nistelrooy

**Hall of FUT**
- Theo Walcott
- Antonio Valencia
- Alessandro Florenzi

**Normal player**
- Éder Militão

That is a pretty deliberate-looking mixture.

If EA only wanted an "all-time legends" event, there would be no obvious reason to combine a normal active player with two Icons and three Hall of FUT names.

But an **Unbreakable event** can do exactly that.

The event becomes the common layer. The player's historical category stays underneath.

That is why I would not describe Hall of FUT as "the new Heroes."

It is much closer to:

**a new historical player category that FC Mobile may use inside regular events and possibly give its own campaigns later.**

## Could Hall of FUT become a major FC Mobile feature?

I think there is a real chance.

Not because the name sounds good, but because EA has already built the idea on the console side.

The concept also solves a problem FC Mobile has had for years: there are plenty of players who are **too famous in FUT culture to feel like ordinary base-card players, but not obvious Icons or Heroes**.

Walcott is the perfect example.

So is Ibarbo.

So is Akinfenwa.

So is Rémy.

EA now has a place to put those names without forcing the Icon or Hero criteria.

That gives EA a much bigger historical player pool to use in events.

## What this could mean for future FC Mobile events

This is the part I would watch closely after Unbreakable launches.

If the Hall of FUT label survives into the live mobile database, we could start seeing:

**Hall of FUT-only Draft pools**

or

**Hall of FUT event chapters**

or

**special Hall of FUT versions inside normal campaigns**

or even

**a dedicated Hall of FUT event later in FC Mobile 27.**

The last option would be especially interesting because the console version already separates Base Hall of FUT items from stronger Campaign versions. citeturn926763search0

FC Mobile could adapt that model rather than copy it exactly.

For example, the mobile version could skip the console's Gallery Token Store and instead use **Draft Vouchers, event currencies, Exchanges or milestone rewards**.

That is only a prediction for now.

There is no official FC Mobile Hall of FUT reward system to point to yet.

## What Hall of FUT is not

A few things are worth clearing up.

**It is not a replacement for Icons.**

EA still uses Icons for the greatest real-world football careers. citeturn926763search1

**It is not the same as Heroes.**

Heroes remain their own historical category with a different real-football focus. citeturn926763search1

**It is not simply "players with good old cards."**

The category is officially built around the memories and impact those players had in Ultimate Team itself. citeturn186672search0

And it is **not confirmed that every Hall of FUT player on console will arrive in FC Mobile**.

The mobile leak only gives us a very strong hint about the direction.

## Hall of FUT vs Icons vs Heroes

| Category | What it celebrates | FC Mobile status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Icons** | Historic real-world football excellence | Established |
| **Heroes** | Memorable real-world club/league careers and moments | Established |
| **Hall of FUT** | **FUT Fame and cult-card history** | **Leaked / not officially explained for mobile** |

That last row is the one to watch.

## Where the current Unbreakable leak fits

The Unbreakable Draft leak now makes much more sense in this context.

The reported pool is not saying:

**"Walcott is an Icon now."**

It is saying:

**"Walcott has a Hall of FUT identity, and FC Mobile may be giving that player an Unbreakable version."**

Same idea for Valencia and Florenzi.

And that is exactly why the leak is more interesting than just another six-player Draft list.

It could be the first real sign that **FC Mobile 27 is starting to import the wider historical card structure from EA FC 27**, rather than relying only on the old Icon/Hero split.

## What we will know after Unbreakable

The live cards should answer the most important questions.

Does the card actually show **Hall of FUT** as its rarity?

Does the player keep that identity in the database?

Can Hall of FUT players appear in other events?

Do they get the same chemistry treatment as another historical category?

Do they appear in Drafts only, or can they be claimed elsewhere?

And, probably the biggest one:

**Will EA give Hall of FUT its own FC Mobile event later?**

Those are the questions worth watching.

For now, there is a clear line between fact and prediction.
