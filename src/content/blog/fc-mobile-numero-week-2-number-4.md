After starting with the iconic No. 7, **FC Mobile Numero moves into Week 2 with Numero 4**, a chapter built around defensive players, defensive structure and some very valuable rewards.

The second chapter went live on **September 3, 2026**, and introduces a new set of activities, a new featured player, new Draft pools and another Numero Kit.

## What Does Number 4 Represent?

In football, the No. 4 is traditionally associated with **defensive stability, positioning and responsibility**.

Rather than being primarily about flair or attacking ability, the number is commonly connected with players who protect the defense, make interceptions and help maintain the team's structure.

That idea is reflected throughout the Numero 4 chapter.

## The Main Reward: 117 OVR Van Dijk

The biggest direct reward in the Numero 4 Puzzle Rewards is **117 OVR CB Virgil van Dijk**.

Players need **700 Numero Tokens** to claim him.

The other major rewards are:

| Reward | Cost |
| --- | ---: |
| 6M Coins + 1,500 Gems | 400 Tokens |
| 500 Rank Up Points | 500 Tokens |
| 2 FC Draft Vouchers | 500 Tokens |
| 1,000 Star Shards | 600 Tokens |
| **117 OVR CB Van Dijk** | **700 Tokens** |

The full set of major rewards requires around **2,700 Numero Tokens** if you want to claim everything.

## Daily Training and 4 Walls

Week 2 changes the event activities to match the No. 4 theme.

### Daily Training

Players can play the available Star Skill Games and matches in Daily Training to earn **Numero Tokens**.

There is a limit of **one round per day**, with a total limit of seven rounds for the chapter.

### 4 Walls

The second special activity is **4 Walls**.

The objective is simple:

**Complete four matches without conceding a goal.**

Successfully completing the activity provides additional Numero Tokens.

It is a nice thematic change from the first week's activities because the challenge actually reflects what the No. 4 represents: defending your goal.

## The 4hield

Week 2 also introduces a temporary login reward section called **The 4hield**.

You simply need to log in on four different days.

| Login | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Day 1 | 4s Profile Banner |
| Day 2 | 444 Rank Up Points |
| Day 3 | 114–119 OVR Numero Player |
| Day 4 | 444 Star Shards |

This is probably the easiest part of Week 2 to complete because it doesn't require significant gameplay grinding.

## Numero 4 Draft

The Draft pools are arguably one of the more interesting parts of Week 2.

### Numero 4 A Draft

- **122 OVR Fernando Hierro**
- **121 OVR Cesc Fàbregas**
- **120 OVR Schlotterbeck**

### Numero 4 B Draft

- **122 OVR Raphaël Varane**
- **121 OVR Ronald Koeman**
- **120 OVR Ronald Araújo**

The pools are noticeably defense-oriented, especially the B Draft, which features three centre-backs.

That gives defensive squads a particularly good reason to use their Draft Vouchers during this chapter.

## Numero Box: Carlos Alberto

There is also a new featured player in the Numero Box for Week 2:

**122 OVR CB Carlos Alberto**

This follows the Week 1 featured player, George Best, and continues the event's focus on famous players connected with the week's number.

## The Bigger Reward: Choose Your Numero

There is an important distinction that players should understand.

The **117 OVR Van Dijk** is the direct Week 2 Puzzle reward.

But the event also has the larger **Choose Your Numero** reward.

To unlock that later, players need to collect all four Numero Kits from the four chapters.

The currently revealed featured choices include:

- **119 OVR ST Dalglish, Rank 4**
- **119 OVR CB Van Dijk, Rank 4**

The other choices will become available as the remaining Numero chapters unlock.

So don't confuse the two Van Dijk cards.

**117 OVR Van Dijk = Week 2 Puzzle Reward**

**119 OVR Van Dijk, Rank 4 = part of the final Choose Your Numero reward**

That distinction matters.

## Is Week 2 Better Than Week 1?

Not necessarily.

Week 1 had **117 OVR Dalglish**, while Week 2 replaces him with **117 OVR Van Dijk**.

The reward structure remains largely the same, but the player type changes completely.

For an attacking squad, Dalglish may have been more useful.

For a team lacking a strong centre-back, Van Dijk could be significantly more valuable.

The Draft is also arguably more attractive for defensive players because of the **122 OVR Hierro and Varane**, alongside Koeman and Araújo.

## Final Verdict

**Numero Week 2 is essentially the defensive chapter of the event.**

The main things to do are straightforward:

1. Complete your Daily Training.
2. Finish the **4 Walls** matches.
3. Log in for all four days of **The 4hield**.
4. Save and spend Numero Tokens wisely.
5. Complete the chapter to obtain the **Numero 4 Kit**.
6. Keep collecting the remaining Kits because the ultimate Choose Your Numero reward requires all four.

The most important part is **not wasting resources just because a new chapter has opened**.

If your team doesn't need Van Dijk, the Coins, Gems, Rank Up Points, Star Shards or Draft Vouchers may provide more practical value. But if your defence is weak, the 117 OVR Van Dijk gives Week 2 a clear purpose.

And remember: **Numero isn't really about choosing one weekly player. The four-week progression is the bigger picture.**
