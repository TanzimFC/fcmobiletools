# FC Mobile 27 Stellar Series: Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius and Future Zodiac Players

**Updated September 27, 2026**

**Future Tracker · FC Mobile 27**

The launch of **Stellar: Libra** introduced a different kind of player series to FC Mobile 27: content built around a Zodiac theme instead of a traditional football event name.

That immediately raises the biggest question:

**Will Libra become a full Stellar Zodiac series?**

Right now, the live game confirms that **Libra exists**, while pre-launch community reporting described additional Zodiac chapters including **Scorpio, Sagittarius and other signs** later in the season.

Those future chapters are not live yet, so they should not be presented as guaranteed releases. This tracker separates what is confirmed, what has been reported, and what remains uncertain.

## Quick status

| Status | Current information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Live now** | Stellar: Libra |
| **Community reports** | More Zodiac chapters |
| **Pattern** | Zodiac timing |
| **Not confirmed** | Future player pools and exact schedules |

# What's confirmed

These points are supported by the live Stellar: Libra event.

## Stellar Libra exists

The first live chapter of the Stellar Series is **Libra**, launched on **September 24, 2026**.

The current chapter introduced:

- Seven Stellar Libra players
- Stellar Draft
- Zodiac branding
- Libra Tokens
- Anniversary integration

Those mechanics are live in FC Mobile 27.

## Libra follows the Zodiac theme

The choice of Libra is closely aligned with the traditional Zodiac calendar.

Libra is generally associated with:

- **September 23**
- through **October 22**

Stellar: Libra launched on September 24, just after the Zodiac period begins.

That timing is one of the clearest reasons players have connected the Stellar name to a recurring Zodiac structure rather than treating Libra as a one-off event label.

# What's been reported

Before launch, FC Mobile community reporting discussed a broader Stellar roadmap.

The reports described Stellar as a continuing Zodiac series rather than a one-time Libra event. The reported sequence included:

- Libra
- Scorpio
- Sagittarius
- Additional Zodiac signs later in the season

Libra becoming a live event makes those earlier reports more relevant, but it does **not** turn every reported future chapter into an official EA announcement.

For now, future signs should be treated as **reported information** until EA or the live game confirms them.

The expectation around Scorpio is mostly based on the pattern created by the first chapter.

The first Stellar chapter already lines up closely with this calendar.

## The event timing also fits

The current Libra event is scheduled through **October 22, 2026**.

That is the final day of the traditional Libra period and is followed immediately by Scorpio's traditional period beginning on October 23.

That creates a neat handoff between the two signs.

However, this is a **pattern**, not confirmation that EA will release Scorpio on October 23.

EA can change event timing, extend content, overlap chapters, or use a completely different live-service schedule.

# Could the currencies change?

The first Stellar chapter introduced two important items:

### Libra Token

The Libra Token is the progression currency attached to the current Libra chapter.

### Stellar Draft Voucher

The Stellar Draft Voucher is the Draft-related item associated with the wider Stellar system.

The names create an interesting distinction.

## Libra Tokens may change with the sign

The progression item is specifically called a **Libra Token**.

That makes it possible that future chapters could use sign-specific versions such as:

- Scorpio Token
- Sagittarius Token
- Capricorn Token

That would be a natural extension of the current naming style.

But it is **not confirmed**.

EA could also keep Libra Tokens active for more than one chapter or redesign the progression system entirely.

## Stellar Draft Voucher may stay series-wide

The Draft item is named **Stellar Draft Voucher**, rather than **Libra Draft Voucher**.

That wording could indicate that it belongs to the wider Stellar system rather than only to the Libra chapter.

A possible structure would therefore be:

**Sign-specific Token → chapter progression**

**Stellar Draft Voucher → shared Stellar Draft system**

That is an interpretation of the current naming, not an official future mechanic.

# Will every Zodiac sign get seven players?

We do not know.

Libra launched with seven identified players, but EA has not confirmed that every future chapter will use the same number.

Future chapters could have:

- Seven players
- More players
- Fewer players
- A completely different reward structure

They could also change the mix of player types.

Potential future formats could include:

- Icons
- Heroes
- Active players
- Mixed player pools
- Different OVR ranges

None of those formats should be treated as confirmed until a future chapter goes live.

# What future signs could look like

The Zodiac concept gives EA a consistent naming framework without requiring every chapter to look identical.

### Scorpio

Traditional period: **October 23 – November 21**

### Sagittarius

Traditional period: **November 22 – December 21**

### Future signs

Capricorn through Virgo could follow afterward if EA continues the series.

The important part is that the **Stellar brand could remain consistent even while the individual sign, player pool and rewards change**.

# How likely is a full Zodiac cycle?

There are several clues that make a larger series possible.

## The strongest clues

- Libra already follows the Zodiac calendar closely.
- The launch date sits almost exactly at the start of Libra.
- The current event runs almost exactly across the Libra period.
- Community reporting described more than one future Zodiac chapter before Libra went live.
- The Draft item uses the broader **Stellar** name rather than a Libra-specific name.

## Reasons to stay cautious

- EA has not published an official twelve-sign roadmap.
- Future player lists are unknown.
- Future Token names are unknown.
- Future voucher distribution is unknown.
- Live-service schedules can move.
- A successful first chapter does not guarantee an identical long-term structure.

The pattern is interesting, but it is still a **pattern rather than an official promise**.

# Confirmed vs reported vs not confirmed

This is the simplest way to follow Stellar without mixing different levels of evidence.

| Category | What belongs here |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Confirmed** | Stellar Libra is live, Libra launched on September 24, Libra uses Zodiac branding, Stellar Draft exists |
| **Reported** | Community reporting describes Scorpio, Sagittarius and additional possible Zodiac chapters |
| **Not confirmed** | Future player lists, exact release dates, future Token names, voucher distribution and future mechanics |

Keeping these categories separate is especially important for a live game because plans can change before a future chapter reaches the live client.

# What would confirm the next chapter?

The strongest confirmation would come from the **live game itself** or a clear official EA announcement.

Useful confirmation signals include:

- A new Stellar sign appearing in the game client
- A new event page with the sign name
- A confirmed player pool
- Official reward information
- A new Draft pool tied to the sign
- An official EA announcement covering the chapter

A community screenshot, leak or social post can be useful evidence, but it should still be labelled as reported until the live game or official EA communication confirms it.
