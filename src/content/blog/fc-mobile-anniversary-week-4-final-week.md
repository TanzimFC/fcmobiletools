The final FC Mobile 3rd Anniversary rotation is taking shape, and the timing matters. Week 4 is expected to run from **October 15 to October 21**, just before the reported Ballon d'Or event on October 22 and a possible rise in the OVR ceiling.

The newest Week 4 reports centre on **Jairzinho, Mario Balotelli and Rudi Völler**. The player pools and the 124 OVR ceiling are community-reported, so check the live game before you commit Draft Vouchers, Coins or Rank Up resources.

## Week 4 Draft players

### Draft A

| Player | Reported OVR | Reported position |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| **David Beckham** | **122** | RM / CM |
| **Emmanuel Petit** | **121** | CDM |
| **Miguel Layún** | **120** | RB / LB |

Beckham is the 122 OVR headline for Draft A. Petit and Layún give the pool more defensive and midfield flexibility, although their final positions and alternate-position options should be checked on the live cards.

### Draft B

| Player | Reported OVR | Reported position |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| **Jairzinho** | **122** | Early lists say RW; newer reveal discussion points to ST |
| **Mario Balotelli** | **121** | ST |
| **Daniel Sturridge** | **120** | ST |

Draft B is heavily attack-focused, with three forwards in the reported pool. Jairzinho's position needs special attention: older leak sheets listed him as a **right wing**, while the more recent reveal discussion describes him as a **striker**. Don't spend on the assumption that he will be a RW or have a particular alternate position until the final item details are visible in-game.

## Rudi Völler is the latest Week 4 Box report

The latest community reports identify **Rudi Völler at 122 OVR, ST** as the Week 4 Anniversary Box player.

That differs from earlier leak sheets that named Gullit and Maldini for the final Box slots. Treat those older names as superseded predictions, not a confirmed final reward list. The in-game Box is the source to trust for its current player, pull rules and cost.

## Mario Balotelli and Hall of FUT

Balotelli is the most unusual reveal in this pool because his card is being identified as a **Hall of FUT** item. He is the first Hall of FUT reveal in the Anniversary lineup, bringing a new card identity into the mobile conversation.

Hall of FUT is not simply another name for Icons or Heroes. The category celebrates players remembered for their impact on Ultimate Team culture: cards that became famous among players for their in-game reputation, distinctive qualities or memorable squad-building history. Balotelli's real-world profile and cult status make him a natural fit for that concept.

Read our [Hall of FUT explainer](/blog/fc-mobile-hall-of-fut-explained/) for the difference between Hall of FUT, Icons and Heroes, and what this category could mean for FC Mobile.

## Jairzinho, not R9

Don't mistake the Brazilian teaser for confirmation that Ronaldo Nazário is coming in this slot. The current Week 4 player reports point to **Jairzinho**, not R9.

The confusion comes from the Brazilian theme and disagreement over the position shown in early leaks versus the latest reveal discussion. Jairzinho's final position, alternate positions and PlayStyles are the details to verify when the card becomes available.

## Ballon d'Or on October 22: is the OVR cap rising to 124?

The Ballon d'Or event is **expected on October 22**. Community reports also point to a **two-point OVR ceiling increase, from 122 to 124**. EA has not confirmed that ceiling change in the official anniversary announcement, so treat it as a planning possibility rather than a guarantee.

That rumour is enough to make resource timing worth thinking about.

- **Hold non-essential Rank Up Points and expensive upgrades** if your current squad is performing well and you want to see what the next OVR tier brings.
- **Don't automatically skip Jairzinho or Balotelli.** If a card fits your formation or fixes a weak position, its actual stats, PlayStyles and gameplay value matter more than the base OVR alone.
- **Use resources with an expiry date before they expire.** Check Draft Vouchers, event currencies and reset timers in-game; never assume a ticket will carry into the next event.
- **Wait for the real Ballon d'Or cards and prices** before making a big Coins or FC Points decision based only on the 124 OVR report.

If the cap does rise, higher-base-OVR cards may become the new targets. But the value of any upgrade still depends on your squad and the cost of getting it.
