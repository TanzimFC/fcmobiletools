The 119 OVR Donyell Malen card briefly let FC Mobile players turn 500 Star Shards into 1,500. That meant a net profit of 1,000 shards per card. The exchange error has since been fixed, but posts showing huge balances are still circulating.

## What happened

On October 15, Malen appeared in Star Signings for 500 Star Shards instead of his reported usual price of 6,000. Players could then exchange the card for 1,500 shards. The game was paying back three times the purchase cost.

Community reports describe a short window before the prices were corrected. Players gave different estimates for how long it lasted, and EA has not published an official timeline.

## The profit math

The mistake was simple: 1,500 shards returned minus the 500 spent equals 1,000 shards gained per card.

- Buying 100 Malen cards at 500 shards cost 50,000 shards.
- Exchanging those cards returned 150,000 shards.
- The net gain was 100,000 shards.

Players who kept reinvesting their balance could repeat the cycle while the incorrect prices were live. That is why community posts quickly started reporting six-figure gains.

## Is the glitch fixed?

Yes. Reports after the correction show Malen's Star Signings price back at 6,000 shards, while the exchange return is 1,500. At those values, each purchase followed by an exchange loses 4,500 shards. It is no longer profitable.

Do not buy Malen expecting to repeat the glitch. The numbers no longer work in the player's favour.

## What about the 2.3 million shard claims?

Some players are now flexing balances above 2.3 million Star Shards. Those claims need evidence. Players commonly cite a balance ceiling of around 1,077,000 shards, but I could not find an official EA source confirming that exact cap as of October 16, 2026.

If that reported ceiling applies, a single live balance of 2.3 million would be above the normal limit. A cropped screenshot or social post is not enough to verify it. The useful proof is an uncropped recording of the live game showing the balance, not a repost with no account context.

## Did players keep the extra shards?

Players who completed exchanges before the correction could make the reported 1,000-shard profit per card at the time. EA has not published a clear public statement confirming whether those balances will stay, be removed, or lead to compensation. Claims about a rollback or rewards for players who missed the window remain speculation.
