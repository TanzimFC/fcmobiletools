FC Mobile 27 is almost here.

EA has confirmed that the next major FC Mobile season update arrives on **September 24, 2026**, alongside the game's third anniversary celebration. Unlike a normal season reset, FC Mobile 27 is built around continuing the existing game while adding new systems, updated squads, new kits, gameplay changes and anniversary content.

This article brings together what EA has officially announced or demonstrated in the FC Mobile 27 beta **as of September 23, 2026**. It also separates confirmed information from things that have appeared in testing but have not received a complete launch specification.

## FC Mobile 27 release date

The FC Mobile 27 Season Update is scheduled for **September 24, 2026**.

The date is tied directly to the third anniversary of EA SPORTS FC Mobile. EA says the anniversary celebration begins with the Season 27 update and will run across a month of anniversary content.

The update is therefore not just a routine roster refresh. EA has positioned it as a major seasonal update with gameplay additions, new systems and anniversary content arriving together.

## The biggest change: there is no season reset

One of the most important confirmed details is that **FC Mobile 27 will not have a traditional season reset**.

EA's official FC Mobile Game Info Hub preview specifically listed "No Season Reset for 27" among the features on the way. That means the current progression is being carried forward instead of forcing players to start their squad from scratch for the new season.

This matters because FC Mobile 27 is not structured like the original 2023 transition into EA SPORTS FC Mobile, where the game moved into a new season with a reset-based progression system.

For FC Mobile 27, the starting point is your existing club.

### What the no-reset structure means

The important distinction is that **no season reset does not mean nothing changes**.

EA is still introducing:

- Updated player squads
- New kits
- Gameplay changes
- New PlayStyles
- Game Plans
- Gauntlet Mode
- A new authentic stadium
- Anniversary content
- New player items and event content
- Further changes tied to the new football season

So your existing club continues, while the game itself receives a substantial new layer of content.

EA has not published a single complete public document covering every possible effect of the no-reset structure on every existing item, market value or progression system. Those details should be checked against the live game after the update rather than assumed.

## Game Plans are the biggest gameplay system coming to FC Mobile 27

EA's September 22 Game Plans Deep Dive confirms one of the central features of the new season.

**Game Plans allow you to change your formation and mentality during a match.**

Previously, tactical changes in FC Mobile were more limited. With Game Plans, you can prepare different tactical setups before a match and switch between them while playing.

EA describes the system as a way to combine formations with different mentalities so that your approach can change depending on the match situation.

For example, a player could start with a balanced setup and then move into a more attacking formation when chasing a goal. The same system can be used in the opposite direction when protecting a lead.

### Five mentalities

FC Mobile 27 expands the mentality system to five options:

1. **Ultra Attacking**
2. **Attacking**
3. **Balanced**
4. **Defensive**
5. **Ultra Defensive**

This is more granular than the previous three-mentality setup.

The important part is that mentality is now tied directly to the Game Plan system. A saved Game Plan is not simply a different formation. It is a formation plus a mentality.

### How many Game Plans can you make?

EA's latest Game Plans Deep Dive says you can create **up to four Game Plans per lineup**.

EA's September 22 Deep Dive contains an inconsistency: its introduction mentions five Game Plans per Squad, while the setup instructions say you can create up to four Game Plans per lineup. Because the detailed setup instructions and the August beta documentation both specify four, we are treating **four Game Plans per lineup as the currently documented figure** until EA clarifies the discrepancy.

This is a useful example of why beta information should not automatically be treated as the final specification.

### What changes on the pitch?

Game Plans are designed to change both your formation and tactical mentality during a match.

EA's example is straightforward: you might want a more aggressive setup when you need a goal, or a more defensive structure when you are trying to protect a result.

The system should therefore make tactical management more important because players can prepare for multiple match situations before kickoff instead of relying entirely on one formation.

The exact competitive impact will depend on how the final live implementation behaves after launch.

## Gauntlet Mode

Gauntlet Mode was introduced in the FC Mobile 27 Limited Beta.

It is a challenge mode built around a series of rounds. Instead of simply playing an ordinary sequence of matches, each round can introduce its own objectives, traits and House Rules.

There is also an important squad-management restriction:

**Player Items have a limited number of appearances.**

That means the mode is designed to make squad depth matter. You cannot necessarily rely on the same group of players throughout the entire challenge.

### How Gauntlet progression works

The beta structure works around **Stars**.

Players progress through the Gauntlet by winning matches and earning Stars, which unlock rewards.

The important pieces confirmed by EA are:

- Multiple rounds
- Different match objectives
- Traits
- House Rules
- Limited player appearances
- Stars for progression
- Rewards tied to Gauntlet progress

EA has not published a complete final reward table for the live Season 27 version in the material available before launch, so specific player rewards, pack contents and final reward quantities should not be presented as confirmed yet.

## Four new PlayStyles

The FC Mobile 27 Limited Beta introduced four additional PlayStyles.

These are:

### Incisive Through Pass

This PlayStyle improves through-ball behavior, including target selection and the distance of the lead pass.

### Dead Ball

Dead Ball improves free-kick characteristics including curl, pace, dip and control.

### Driven Lob Through

This PlayStyle affects lobbed through balls, with improvements to curve, control and lead distance.

### Slide Tackle

Slide Tackle increases slide-tackle range and speeds up tackling animations.

These are additions to the broader PlayStyles system that EA introduced to FC Mobile earlier in 2026.

PlayStyles are designed to make selected players behave differently on the pitch rather than relying only on their numerical attributes.

## PlayStyles are now part of the wider FC Mobile system

The four FC Mobile 27 beta PlayStyles should not be confused with the original PlayStyles introduction.

EA first tested PlayStyles in an April 2026 Limited Beta, with 18 PlayStyles covering areas such as passing, finishing, physical play, defending, ball control and goalkeeping.

The May 2026 PlayStyles Deep Dive then brought the system into the main FC Mobile experience.

FC Mobile 27 adds another four tested PlayStyles:

| PlayStyle | Main effect |
|---|---|
| Incisive Through Pass | Better through-ball targeting and lead distance |
| Dead Ball | Improved free-kick curl, pace, dip and control |
| Driven Lob Through | Improved lobbed through-ball curve, control and lead distance |
| Slide Tackle | Greater slide-tackle range and faster tackle animations |

The practical question for players will be which Player Items receive these PlayStyles and how their effects interact with attributes in the live version.
