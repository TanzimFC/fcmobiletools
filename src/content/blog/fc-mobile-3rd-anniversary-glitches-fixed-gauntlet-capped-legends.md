**The FC Mobile 3rd Anniversary problems that caused the most confusion are already being cleaned up: the Gauntlet quest issue can now be fixed by resetting the Gauntlet, and the capped Legends glitch was also fixed today.**

The original problems looked worse than they ended up being.

Players who had already completed the **3rd Anniversary Gauntlet** could get stuck on a quest asking them to earn stars from a mode they had already finished. That left the quest sitting there even though the player had effectively done the required content.

That issue has now been addressed.

## Reset the Gauntlet

The fix for players who already played the Gauntlet is simple.

**Reset the 3rd Anniversary Gauntlet.**

After the reset, the affected quest can be completed normally.

That is the part players needed. There is no reason to replay the old state of the mode or assume the quest is permanently locked.

The problem came from the way the quest checked Gauntlet progress. Players who had already cleared it could not generate the new star progress the quest was looking for. Resetting the Gauntlet gives the game a fresh state to work with.

For anyone opening the Anniversary quests and seeing the Gauntlet requirement still sitting there, the first thing to try is the reset.

## The Anniversary token glitch is fixed

The **capped Legends glitch and the Anniversary Token repeat-reward glitch were the same issue**.

The bug allowed a completed skill game to behave as though its reward had not already been claimed, letting players receive **3rd Anniversary Tokens** again instead of the activity staying permanently completed.

That is why the issue was described in different ways across player reports, including the capped Legends glitch.

![FC Mobile 3rd Anniversary capped Legends glitch](https://res.cloudinary.com/b0qikv7n/image/upload/v1791482667/fc-mobile-tools/sugr6bzu0pdorctbwqaz.jpg)

The issue has now been **fixed**, with the fix landing **roughly two hours ago** at the time of this update.

There is no point building a strategy around the old repeat-reward behaviour now.

I also did **not use the token trick myself**. I simply did not get the same chance to play that some other players had before the issue was fixed.

So I am not going to pretend I personally farmed thousands of Anniversary Tokens.

## Anniversary Tokens and the rewards

The exploit attracted attention because it could accelerate access to Anniversary rewards, but I did not personally find a huge amount of use for the Anniversary Tokens themselves.

There were still rewards available through the Anniversary content, including **Gems and other rewards**.

The important distinction is between the normal event rewards and what players could get by repeatedly triggering the same reward because of the glitch.

## 10,000 Gems for 100 games

There was also discussion around the rewards available from playing **100 games**.

The **10,000 Gems reward for 100 games is confirmed**.

So the Gem figure itself does not need to be treated as a rumour. The part that varies is whether an individual player actually had the chance to take advantage of the related glitch before it was fixed.

That is a very different claim from saying that everyone automatically received 10,000 Gems.

## Anniversary Tokens had limited value outside the affected rewards

The token discussion also needs a little context.

There was not a huge amount I personally needed to spend Anniversary Tokens on, and I did not use the exploit to stockpile them. The Anniversary content did offer rewards and some Gem value, but that is different from saying there was a guaranteed massive payout waiting behind the glitch.

The reported token farming mainly mattered because it could accelerate access to Anniversary rewards.

That is why the bug attracted so much attention.

It was not because every token had some special long-term use.

## League Gems: my account had no problem

The League Gems situation is harder to generalize.

There were reports from players who were having trouble with League Gems. I cannot speak for every account because I did not see the same behaviour myself.

**My League Gems were fine.**

That includes last week. I did not have an issue receiving them when other players were reporting problems.

So I am leaving that one as an account-dependent report rather than declaring that the problem is either completely solved or completely broken for everyone.

The only safe statement from my own experience is that **I did not experience the League Gems issue**.

## The Anniversary event is moving quickly

This is why old glitch guides become risky so fast in FC Mobile.

A method can work for a few hours, spread through the community, and then stop working after a server-side change or a game-state reset. That is exactly what happened with the Anniversary issues.

So the useful information now is not an old token-farming walkthrough.

It is the current state of the fixes.

**Reset the Gauntlet** if the Anniversary quest is stuck because you already completed the mode.

The **capped Legends glitch is fixed**.

The **10,000 Gem reward for 100 games is confirmed**.

And on League Gems, my experience still does not match the reports from players who were affected.
