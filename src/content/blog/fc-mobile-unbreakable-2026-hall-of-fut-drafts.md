**FC Mobile Unbreakable is expected to start on October 15**, the same day as the reported CONMEBOL Libertadores event.

The first Unbreakable Draft leak is already causing a different kind of discussion, though. The reported players include retired stars such as **Javier Zanetti** and **Ruud van Nistelrooy**, even though they are not being presented as the familiar Icons or Heroes players many FC Mobile fans are used to seeing.

That raises a very fair question:

**What exactly are these cards?**

## Unbreakable Drafts: 122 OVR at the top

The reported first Draft pools are:

### Draft A

| Player | OVR | Position |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| **Javier Zanetti** | **122** | RB |
| **Theo Walcott** | **121** | RW |
| **Éder Militão** | **120** | CB |

### Draft B

| Player | OVR | Position |
| :--- | ---: | --- |
| **Ruud van Nistelrooy** | **122** | ST |
| **Antonio Valencia** | **121** | RM |
| **Alessandro Florenzi** | **120** | RB |

The list comes from **MadridistaaFC**, and several FC Mobile community trackers are reporting the same six names. The leak is still unofficial, so EA can change the players, OVRs, positions or Draft structure before launch.

## Why Zanetti and van Nistelrooy are causing confusion

This is the interesting part of the leak.

A player like **Éder Militão** is easy to understand because he is an active footballer. **Walcott, Zanetti, van Nistelrooy and Valencia** are different.

They are retired players, but the leaked cards are not being described simply as normal Icons or Heroes.

That is why the community question around the leak matters. It is not really about whether EA can put retired players into an event. EA already does that.

The question is whether FC Mobile is now getting another **player-item tier**.

And there is a very strong reason to think that is possible.

## Hall of FUT is a real new card type in FC 27

EA officially introduced **Hall of FUT** for EA SPORTS FC 27 on July 23, 2026.

EA describes Hall of FUT as a **new Player Item type** for players who became iconic within Ultimate Team itself. EA also makes a point of saying these players might not be ICONS. The first announced class included **Adebayo Akinfenwa, Marouane Fellaini, Loïc Rémy, Eljero Elia and Giovani dos Santos**. citeturn867024search0

That distinction is important.

**Icons** are built around legendary real-world careers and historic status.

**Heroes** focus more on unforgettable football moments, club or league spells, and the impact a player had on fans.

**Hall of FUT** is different. It is about the player's legacy inside FUT and the memories their cards created for the community. EA has positioned it as its own Player Item type rather than quietly folding these names into the existing Icon or Hero categories. citeturn554197search3turn867024search0

That explains why a player who was never an Icon can still make sense in Hall of FUT.

## So are the Unbreakable cards Hall of FUT?

**That is the big question, and right now we should not call it confirmed on mobile.**

The leaked Unbreakable pool strongly points in that direction, especially because the community has already been reporting Hall of FUT cards appearing in FC Mobile content. Some leak trackers have specifically linked names such as **Theo Walcott** and **Antonio Valencia** to the new Hall of FUT category. citeturn329368search1turn329368search8

But EA has **not yet published an official FC Mobile announcement saying that Unbreakable is introducing Hall of FUT as a mobile card type**.

So there are two separate facts here:

**Hall of FUT is officially real in FC 27.**

**Its exact FC Mobile implementation is not officially explained yet.**

That is why calling every leaked Unbreakable retired player a confirmed Hall of FUT card would be too early.

## What Unbreakable might be doing with it

The theme gives us another clue.

The original FC 26 Unbreakables campaign on console was built around players who overcame setbacks, injuries and difficult career situations. It included current players plus selected Heroes and Icons, along with special Evolutions and campaign content. citeturn554197image1

FC Mobile appears to be taking its own route with the same broader idea.

Look at the leaked Draft A:

**Zanetti → Walcott → Militão**

Then Draft B:

**van Nistelrooy → Valencia → Florenzi**

That is not a normal current-player-only promotion. It mixes active players with older fan-favourite names.

That fits a format where **Unbreakable is the event theme**, while the actual Player Item type can vary.

So the cleanest way to think about it right now is:

**Unbreakable = the event.**

**Hall of FUT = potentially one of the card types inside that event.**

They are not necessarily the same thing.

## Why Hall of FUT could be a big deal on mobile

This is where things get interesting for FC Mobile.

The game has traditionally had a familiar hierarchy for retired players: **Icons and Heroes**.

Hall of FUT could give EA another way to bring back players who have a huge FC Mobile or FUT following but do not fit the normal Icon or Hero criteria.

Think about the type of players this opens the door for.

A player can be remembered because his old card was ridiculously usable, because everyone knew his pace, because a strange combination of PlayStyles made him a community favourite, or simply because he was one of those cards everyone used during a particular FUT era.

That is almost a different kind of football legacy.

And the first Unbreakable leaks are exactly the sort of place where this new category could start showing up.

## What we still don't know

There are some big unanswered questions for the mobile version.

We do not yet have an official EA explanation for whether Hall of FUT cards on mobile will:

- have their own card design
- follow the same chemistry rules as Heroes
- receive special PlayStyle treatment
- appear only in certain events or throughout the season
- be available through Drafts, Packs, Exchanges or other routes
- count exactly like Icons and Heroes for squad-building purposes

EA has already confirmed that Hall of FUT is a separate item type on the FC 27 side, but the mobile rules are still the part we need to see. citeturn554197search6turn867024search0

That is why this Unbreakable leak is more interesting than just another list of 120-122 OVR cards.

## Unbreakable and Libertadores arrive together

There is another thing worth watching on October 15.

**Unbreakable and CONMEBOL Libertadores are both expected to arrive on the same day, with 122 OVR reported as the top rating for both events.**

That is a lot of content landing at once.

For F2P players, the resource question becomes real. You have two event tracks competing for your Draft Vouchers, Gems and time, while Ballon d'Or is expected to follow on October 22.

The smart move is to wait for the live reward structure before burning everything.

The player names are exciting. The actual cost of getting them matters more.

## What to watch when Unbreakable goes live

The first thing to check is the **card badge or item classification**.

That should answer the Hall of FUT question faster than any leak.

Then look at **PlayStyles, Rank-Up requirements, positions and the Draft rules**. A 122 OVR Zanetti can be a huge card for Team OVR, but his real value will depend on how EA builds the item and how easy it is to obtain and Rank Up.

For now, though, the leak has already given us one clear headline:

**122 OVR Javier Zanetti and 122 OVR Ruud van Nistelrooy are reportedly coming through the Unbreakable Drafts.**

And the fact that these retired names are appearing outside the usual Icon and Hero conversation may be the first big sign that **Hall of FUT is about to become part of the FC Mobile ecosystem too**.
