**122 OVR Beckham leads Chapter 8, while Chapter 9 reportedly brings a completely different set of card concepts, including Gemini Verse, Swap Verse and a 117 OVR Rio Ferdinand with two visual versions.**

The latest leak adds details for **Chapter 8: Glorious Eras** and **Chapter 9: Footyverse** of the FC Mobile 3rd Anniversary content.

This is leak information. The entries below are limited to the player names, OVRs and card systems currently reported. Where the leak does not name a player, this article does not fill the gap with guesses.

## Chapter 8: Glorious Eras

Chapter 8 is labelled **Glorious Eras**.

The reported headline is:

| Card system | Player | OVR |
| :--- | :--- | ---: |
| **Card Design Exchange** | **David Beckham** | **122** |

That is the only player entry included in the Chapter 8 leak supplied here.

There is no reported position, exchange requirement or additional reward detail in the current information, so none is being added to the article.

## Chapter 9: Footyverse

Chapter 9 is where the card systems multiply.

The chapter is labelled **Footyverse**, with Gemini Verse A, Gemini Verse B, Swap Verse, Player Morphoverse and another Card Design Exchange entry reported.

### Gemini Verse A

The first Gemini Verse group is listed at **117 OVR**:

| Card system | Player | OVR |
| :--- | :--- | ---: |
| **Gemini Verse A** | **Bukayo Saka** | **117** |
| **Gemini Verse A** | **George** | **117** |

The leak does not provide positions or any additional card statistics for either player.

### Gemini Verse B

The second Gemini Verse group is also listed at **117 OVR**:

| Card system | Player | OVR |
| :--- | :--- | ---: |
| **Gemini Verse B** | **Bryan Mbeumo** | **117** |
| **Gemini Verse B** | **Mazraoui** | **117** |

Again, the current leak does not specify positions or any extra attributes.

## Swap Verse

The latest report also lists a **Swap Verse** with cards at:

**118 OVR**  
**119 OVR**

The player names attached to those ratings have not been provided in the leak information used for this article.

That leaves the correct status as **118 and 119 OVR Swap Verse cards, players not yet specified**.

Nothing more should be assumed from the ratings alone.

## Rio Ferdinand gets the Morphoverse treatment

One of the more unusual entries is the **3rd Anniversary Player Morphoverse** card.

The reported player is:

**Rio Ferdinand — 117 OVR**

The leak also identifies two looks for the card:

**Cornrows**  
**Skinhead**

That is the full reported detail.

There is no information here confirming whether the two looks affect attributes, gameplay, position, skill moves, work rates or anything else. They should therefore be treated as **visual variants** in the current leak report, not as separate gameplay cards.

## Card Design Exchange returns with Petit

Chapter 9 also reportedly includes a second **Card Design Exchange** entry.

This one is:

**Emmanuel Petit — 121 OVR**

That places Petit in **Footyverse**, alongside the Gemini Verse and Morphoverse content.

The chapter therefore has two reported Card Design Exchange players across the latest leak set: **David Beckham at 122 OVR in Chapter 8** and **Emmanuel Petit at 121 OVR in Chapter 9**.

Their exchange requirements are not included in the current leak.

## What the leak actually tells us

The cleanest takeaway is the split between the two chapters.

**Chapter 8: Glorious Eras** currently has a reported **122 OVR David Beckham** through Card Design Exchange.

**Chapter 9: Footyverse** has a broader mix of systems: **Gemini Verse A**, **Gemini Verse B**, **Swap Verse**, **Player Morphoverse** and **Card Design Exchange**.

The named OVRs in this leak run from **117 to 122**.

But the numbers alone do not tell us which card is strongest for a squad. The leak gives no attributes, positions, work rates, skill moves or acquisition requirements for these entries.

So there is no sensible reason to rank Saka against Mbeumo, compare Rio Ferdinand with Petit in gameplay, or claim that the 119 OVR Swap Verse cards will be better than the 117 OVR Morphoverse card.

Those details are not in the leak.

For now, the confirmed reported entries from this leak set are **122 OVR Beckham in Chapter 8**, followed by **117 OVR Saka and George, 117 OVR Mbeumo and Mazraoui, 118–119 OVR Swap Verse cards, 117 OVR Rio Ferdinand with Cornrows and Skinhead looks, and 121 OVR Petit in Chapter 9**.
