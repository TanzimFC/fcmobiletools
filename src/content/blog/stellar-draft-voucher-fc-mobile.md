The **Stellar Draft Voucher** is a special event item introduced with **Stellar: Libra** in FC Mobile 27. It is tied to the new Stellar Draft system and is separate from the regular Draft Voucher players already know from FC Mobile.

The important part is that the live event has created confusion around which voucher is actually being consumed. Players have reported cases where a Stellar Draft entry reduces their normal Draft Voucher balance instead of the Stellar-specific voucher they expected to use.

This guide explains the voucher clearly, without mixing it up with Libra Tokens or the normal Draft Voucher.

## What is the Stellar Draft Voucher?

<div class="article-inline-icon"><img src="https://res.cloudinary.com/b0qikv7n/image/upload/v1790528955/fc-mobile-tools/kac9t1aomu9fohcx9ln7.png" alt="Stellar Draft Voucher icon" loading="lazy" style="width:96px;max-width:96px;height:96px;max-height:96px;object-fit:contain;margin:18px 0 8px;border:0;background:transparent;box-shadow:none" /></div>

The Stellar Draft Voucher is the **special ticket associated with the Stellar Draft**.

It was introduced for the Stellar Series, beginning with the Libra chapter. The purpose is to separate the new Zodiac-themed draft content from the game's standard Draft system.

In simple terms:

| Item | Main role |
| --- | --- |
| **Stellar Draft Voucher** | Entry item for the Stellar Draft system |
| **Regular Draft Voucher** | Entry item for the standard Draft system |
| **Stellar Libra Token** | Event progression and reward currency |

The three items are different. A Libra Token is not a Draft Voucher, and a regular Draft Voucher is not the same item as the Stellar Draft Voucher.

## Where is the Stellar Draft?

The Stellar Draft is part of the live Stellar: Libra content in FC Mobile 27.

Depending on the current event layout, players may encounter Stellar content through the event and Draft interfaces connected to the anniversary period. The important thing is to look for the **Stellar** or **Stellar: Libra** label rather than assuming every Draft screen uses the same voucher.

The Libra chapter currently has its own player pool, with cards ranging from **120 OVR to 122 OVR**.

## Stellar Draft Voucher vs regular Draft Voucher

The easiest way to understand the difference is to treat them as two separate tickets.

**Stellar Draft Voucher**

Created for the new Stellar Draft system and associated with the Stellar Series.

**Regular Draft Voucher**

Used for the normal FC Mobile Draft system outside the Stellar-specific setup.

That distinction matters because vouchers can represent real in-game value. Using the wrong voucher can change how many future Draft attempts you have available.

## The current voucher-consumption issue

The biggest problem around the Stellar Draft Voucher is not the idea of the item itself. It is the way the live Draft can behave on some accounts.

Players have reported that a Stellar Draft attempt can reduce the number of **regular Draft Vouchers** they own.

That is important because the expected behavior is much easier to understand if the Stellar Draft is treated as its own event system. If the game takes a normal Draft Voucher instead, players can lose a resource they were saving for the standard Draft system.

At the moment, this should be treated as a **live implementation issue**, not as a reason to assume every account behaves identically.

## How to check which voucher you are losing

Before making several Draft attempts, check your balances.

1. Note your current regular Draft Voucher count.
2. Note whether the Stellar Draft screen shows a Stellar-specific voucher requirement.
3. Enter the Draft once.
4. Recheck both balances after the Draft completes.

This simple check helps you identify what your own account is consuming while the event is still being adjusted.

## Should you spend your vouchers immediately?

There is no universal answer because the value depends on which balance your account is actually using and what you are trying to save for.

The useful approach is to **check the cost shown by the live Draft screen before every attempt**.

Do not assume that a voucher shown in an earlier screenshot or video will behave exactly the same way after an event-side change.

## Stellar Draft Voucher and Libra Tokens are not the same

This is one of the easiest mistakes to make.

The **Stellar Draft Voucher** is a Draft-related item.

The **Stellar Libra Token** belongs to the event's progression and reward system.

For example:

**Voucher → Draft entry**

**Token → Event progression**

Having Libra Tokens does not automatically give you Draft attempts, and having Draft Vouchers does not automatically give you Libra Tokens.

## Will the Stellar Draft Voucher stay after Libra?

The Stellar Draft Voucher is currently tied to the Stellar Series rather than being presented as a permanent everyday currency.

Because Stellar: Libra is a limited event chapter, players should treat the voucher as **event-specific** unless the live game later confirms a broader use.

Future Stellar chapters may change how the same item is used, but exact future voucher behavior should not be assumed before those chapters are live.

## Common mistakes to avoid

### Confusing the voucher with Libra Tokens

They have different purposes.

### Assuming every Draft uses the same ticket

The Stellar Draft is a separate event system, so always check the live requirement.

### Spending without checking your balance

The current voucher-consumption issue makes this especially important.

### Treating old screenshots as current

FC Mobile event screens can change after maintenance or server-side updates.

## Quick answer

**What is the Stellar Draft Voucher?**  
A special voucher associated with the Stellar Draft and Stellar Series.

**Is it the same as a regular Draft Voucher?**  
No. They are separate items.

**What is happening with the voucher right now?**  
Players have reported cases where Stellar Draft attempts consume regular Draft Vouchers.

**Should I assume every account has the same behavior?**  
No. Check the balances shown on your own live account.

**Are Libra Tokens the same thing?**  
No. Libra Tokens are event progression currency.
