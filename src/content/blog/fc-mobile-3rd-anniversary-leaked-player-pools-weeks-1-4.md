## Draft vs Anniversary Box: the spending difference

This is where players need to read the event structure carefully.

The **FC Draft pools are tied to FC Draft Vouchers**.

The **Anniversary Box works differently**. The reported Box has **12 total reward slots**, the **first pull is free**, and the remaining attempts use **FC Points**.

So the two systems are not interchangeable.

A player who has been saving Draft Vouchers is looking at the Draft pools. A player deciding whether to spend FC Points is looking at the Box.

That distinction is especially important for **F2P players**. A free first Box attempt is useful, but the next 11 attempts are a different conversation because they require FC Points.

## How the reported Anniversary Box works

The reported Box structure is straightforward.

### First attempt: free

Every player gets the first Box attempt without spending FC Points.

That makes the first pull the obvious no-cost look at the current Box.

### The other 11 slots cost FC Points

After the free attempt, the remaining **11 reward slots** require FC Points.

The exact FC Point cost is not included in the leak details here, so do not build a spending plan around a guessed number.

### No duplicates

The reported structure also says that once a player or item is drawn, it is removed from the remaining Box rewards.

That means there are no duplicate pulls inside the same Box clear.

The practical result is important:

**If you clear all 12 slots, you are guaranteed to get the featured 122 OVR player in that Box.**

That is a very different mechanic from a normal pack where the same high-end card can keep missing you over and over.

## What the no-duplicate Box rule means for F2P players

This is probably the most useful part of the leak to understand before spending anything.

Do not look at a 122 OVR Box card and immediately compare it with the highest OVR player on the Draft board. The acquisition method is different.

For an F2P account, the decision is mainly about the **free first attempt** and whether the rest of the Box can realistically fit your FC Points budget.

For a player who already spends FC Points, the no-duplicate structure makes the Box easier to model because the reward pool shrinks after every pull.

That does not automatically make every Box worth clearing. It just means the spending outcome is more predictable than a pure repeatable pack system.
