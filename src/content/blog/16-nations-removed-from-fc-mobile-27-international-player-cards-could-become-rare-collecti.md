FC Mobile 27 could be taking away one of the most unusual collections of player cards from the current game.

Based on FC27 beta data and the national-team changes seen around the new season, **16 nations featured in The World’s Game could be removed from the international side of FC Mobile 27**. That would affect more than the national-team tournament itself. Players who own certain international Player Items could end up holding cards that are no longer obtainable through normal game systems.

And there is an important detail many players could miss:

**The player’s nationality is not the same thing as the national-team card.**

A player being from a particular country does not mean every card of that player will disappear.

## The 16 nations reportedly being removed

The following 16 national teams have been identified in FC27 data as removed compared with the previous setup:

| # | Nation |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Côte d’Ivoire |
| 2 | Senegal |
| 3 | Cape Verde |
| 4 | Algeria* |
| 5 | Ecuador |
| 6 | Canada |
| 7 | Bosnia & Herzegovina |
| 8 | Iran* |
| 9 | Iraq* |
| 10 | Tunisia |
| 11 | Panama |
| 12 | Curaçao* |
| 13 | South Africa |
| 14 | Haiti |
| 15 | Uzbekistan |
| 16 | Jordan* |

*The starred nations were represented with generic national teams in the relevant FC27 data rather than fully licensed national-team implementations. The 16-team list comes from community analysis of FC27 data rather than an EA-published FC Mobile announcement.

This is especially notable because several of these nations were part of FC Mobile's **The World’s Game Tournament**, which introduced more than 50 national teams and gave them national kits, histories, ratings and special flag-themed Player Items.

## Cape Verde could be the biggest shock

For FC Mobile collectors, **Cape Verde is one of the most interesting names on the list**.

Cape Verde was included in The World’s Game Tournament, and the mode specifically gave national teams their own international identity and Player Items.

That means cards connected specifically to Cape Verde's international team could become much harder to find once FC Mobile 27 moves away from that player pool.

One obvious example is **Vozinha**.

Vozinha has become a familiar name among FC Mobile players because of the special cards he has received. But the important distinction is that **not every Vozinha card is automatically affected**.

A normal club-based Vozinha card and a Cape Verde international Player Item are two different things.

If the Cape Verde international card is removed from the FC Mobile 27 pool, that specific international version could become a legacy card.

## Your player's nationality is not being deleted

This is where the change can easily be misunderstood.

Imagine a player has:

**Club card:** Player X playing for his club
**Nationality:** Cape Verde
**International card:** Player X representing Cape Verde

Those are not the same data point.

Removing Cape Verde as a national-team option does **not** mean EA has suddenly removed Cape Verdean nationality from the player database.

It also does not mean every club card belonging to a Cape Verdean player disappears.

The change is primarily about the **national-team version of the Player Item**.

EA's description of The World's Game specifically separates the national-team experience from the regular club-building experience, including flag-themed Player Items for international teams.

## What happens to the international cards you already own?

This is the part collectors should pay attention to.

The expected situation is that an international card from one of these nations could become **unobtainable through the normal FC Mobile 27 player pool** once the relevant database is removed.

In practical terms, that means an existing international card can become much more difficult to acquire after the change.

The most important distinction is:

**Removed from the obtainable pool does not necessarily mean deleted from your account.**

FC Mobile has previously had player-card situations where a card became unavailable on the Market while existing owners retained the card. For example, EA forum users documented retired-player cards disappearing from Market availability while copies already owned remained in accounts. That precedent is not identical to the FC27 national-team change, so the precise FC27 implementation should still be considered unconfirmed until launch.

## Will these cards disappear from the Market?

This is the likely consequence players should watch.

If the international versions are removed from the active FC Mobile 27 database, **new copies should no longer enter the Market through normal sources**.

That would mean:


Existing tradeable copies could remain with their owners.


New copies would no longer be generated normally.


Market supply could eventually fall to zero.


A card could become effectively a legacy item.

However, EA has **not officially published FC Mobile 27 rules saying exactly how these 16 nations' international cards will be handled on the Market**, so this should not be treated as a confirmed Market announcement yet.

## What about Star Signings?

Star Signings is another important area.

If these international Player Items are removed from the FC Mobile 27 player pool, there would be no reason to expect those exact cards to continue appearing as normal Star Signings options after the database transition.

So players should expect the removed international versions to leave the normal acquisition pool rather than continue being regularly offered.

Again, the exact FC27 Star Signings configuration has not been publicly documented by EA, so this is a database-based expectation rather than an official guarantee.

## And Draft?

Draft could be affected for the same reason.

The Draft system pulls from defined player pools. When a card is removed from the underlying obtainable pool, it should no longer appear as a normal Draft option unless EA deliberately keeps that card available as an exception.

That makes the timing important.

A player who wants one of these international cards should not assume that the card will remain obtainable indefinitely just because the player himself still exists in FC Mobile.

## Your existing card could become a collector's item

This is where the situation gets interesting.

Suppose you already own a Cape Verde international Vozinha card.

After the transition, there could be **no normal way to obtain another copy**.

You would still have the card, but its status would be completely different.

It would no longer simply be another card available somewhere in the game. It could become a **legacy international card** from the previous World’s Game setup.

For collectors, that is potentially more interesting than its normal gameplay value.

The same idea could apply to international cards representing Senegal, Côte d’Ivoire, Canada, Bosnia & Herzegovina and the other nations on the list.

## But don't rush to throw these cards into exchanges

This is probably the biggest practical point.

Some players may look at a low-rated international card and think:

> 
> "It's useless. I'll exchange it for coins, shards or another reward."
> 

That could be a mistake from a collecting perspective.

The exchange value of an old, low-OVR card is usually straightforward and limited. Once a card becomes unobtainable, its **historical scarcity** can become the more interesting part.

That does not guarantee that these cards will become expensive or valuable on the Market. There is no basis for promising that.

But once supply is cut off, **you cannot recreate the same card later just because you regret exchanging it**.

So for collectors, keeping a unique international card may make more sense than immediately converting it into a relatively small exchange reward.

## What changes in The World's Game Tournament?

The national-team side is even more obvious.

The World's Game Tournament was built around selecting national teams and playing a knockout competition, with the available nations forming the pool of teams players could choose.

If these nations are absent from the FC Mobile 27 national-team database, their national teams would naturally no longer be available as selectable teams in that mode unless EA adds them back later.

That means Cape Verde, Senegal, Côte d’Ivoire, Canada, South Africa, Tunisia and the other removed nations could disappear from the international tournament experience.

This would be a separate change from club cards.

## Club cards and national-team cards are different

This deserves to be repeated because it is the easiest part to get wrong.

**Club card ≠ nationality ≠ national-team card**

A player can still have:

**Club representation**
The club he plays for.

**Nationality**
The country associated with the player.

**National-team representation**
The special international Player Item/team representation used for the national football experience.

Removing the third does not automatically remove the first two.

So seeing a Cape Verdean player still present in FC Mobile 27 does not contradict the removal of Cape Verde's national-team content.

## What should players do before FC Mobile 27?

The safest move is simple: **check your club and international collections before the season transition**.

Pay particular attention to international Player Items from the 16 affected nations.

A card such as a Cape Verde international Vozinha could become unusual precisely because the player himself remains part of football and his other cards can still exist while that specific international version leaves the obtainable pool.

The difference between "removed from the game" and "removed from the obtainable pool" matters enormously here.

Your card may not vanish.

**The ability to obtain another one may.**

### Important note


The 16-nation removal list is based on FC27 data and community analysis. EA has officially confirmed FC Mobile's international Player Item system and The World's Game national-team structure, but has not, at the time of writing, published a detailed FC Mobile 27 announcement confirming each of these 16 removals and exactly how every Market, Draft, Star Signings and Exchange interaction will work at launch.
