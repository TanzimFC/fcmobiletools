## FC Mobile 27 is here

The FC Mobile 27 Season Update is scheduled for **September 24, 2026**. EA is also launching the game's **third anniversary celebration** with the new season. EA says the season update brings updated squads, new kits and gameplay innovations, alongside a month-long anniversary celebration. citeturn0search9

## Maintenance

**Maintenance starts at 21:30 UTC on September 23.**

EA's FC Mobile account announced the maintenance time and estimated the downtime at about **4.5 hours**. The preview for the update is also scheduled during FC Mobile LIVE at **00:00 UTC**, with the anniversary expected to go live around **02:00 UTC**. citeturn0search11

## What is changing

The FC Mobile 27 beta gave players an early look at several gameplay features coming with the new season:

- **Game Plans:** switch formations and mentalities during a match. The beta supported up to four Game Plans.
- **Gauntlet Mode:** a round-based mode with match objectives, gameplay modifiers and House Rules.
- **Four new PlayStyles:** Incisive Through Pass, Dead Ball, Driven Lob Through and Slide Tackle. citeturn0search5

These features were publicly described by EA for the FC Mobile 27 beta. The final live version can differ from the beta, so beta details should not be treated as a complete final patch note. citeturn0search1turn0search5

## Anniversary

EA's third anniversary event is tied directly to the FC Mobile 27 Season Update. EA says selected players from its community voting campaign will be added on September 24, while selected retro soundtrack songs will also arrive with the update. citeturn0search9
