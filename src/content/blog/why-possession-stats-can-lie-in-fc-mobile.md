Possession looks impressive on a scoreboard, but it does not automatically mean you controlled the match.

## What possession actually tells you

Possession measures how long your side had the ball. It does not tell you whether those touches created dangerous chances, moved the opponent out of shape, or produced a shot from a useful area.

## The better question

Instead of chasing a higher possession percentage, look at what happened while you had the ball: progression, chances created, shots, turnovers and the quality of your attacks.

## The takeaway

Use possession as context, not as a win condition. A smaller share of the ball can still be the better performance when your attacks are more direct and efficient.
