**FC Mobile Numero Week 1** is now live, beginning with one of football's most iconic shirt numbers: **Number 7**.

The first week of Numero focuses on famous players who have worn the number 7, with daily trivia questions, token rewards and additional objectives that allow players to earn valuable resources.

## What Is Numero Week 1?

Numero is a trivia-focused event in FC Mobile where players answer football questions based around a specific shirt number.

For Week 1, the featured number is **7**. The chapter opened on **August 27, 2026**, with the next chapters scheduled to move through different numbers each week.

The weekly structure means players have several days to complete the available trivia and objectives before the event moves on to the next number.

## Week 1: Number 7

Number 7 is one of the most famous shirt numbers in football.

Players such as Cristiano Ronaldo, David Beckham, Luis Figo, George Best and many other legendary footballers have worn the number throughout football history.

That makes the first Numero chapter particularly fitting because the event is built around identifying players and moments connected to the featured number.

## Numero Week 1 Rewards

The trivia section allows players to earn **Numero Tokens**, which can then be exchanged for different rewards.

The main Week 1 exchange rewards include:

| Reward | Token Cost |
| --- | ---: |
| 6,000,000 Coins + 1,500 Gems | 400 |
| 500 Rank Up Points | 500 |
| 2 Draft Vouchers | 500 |
| 1,000 Star Shards | 600 |
| 117 OVR ST Dalglish | 700 |

The standout reward is **117 OVR ST Kenny Dalglish**, making the token-saving decision particularly important for players who want a usable player rather than simply taking the resource rewards.

## Week 1 Objectives

Numero Week 1 also includes special objectives based around the number 7.

Players can progress through the objectives by scoring goals in any Match mode.

The milestones include:

- **7 goals:** 7s Profile Banner
- **77 goals:** 777 Rank Up Points
- **277 goals:** 114–119 OVR Number One Player
- **777 goals:** 777 Star Shards + 7,777 Gems

The final milestone is the biggest reward, but reaching 777 goals requires significantly more grinding than the earlier objectives.

## Numero Week 1 Daily Questions

The trivia questions change throughout the week, meaning players need to return regularly rather than treating Numero as a single-day activity.

The questions are based around football players and facts connected to **Number 7**.

If you are trying to maximize your rewards, the important thing is simple: **don't miss the daily questions**. Even when a single question seems insignificant, the accumulated tokens can determine whether you reach the higher-value exchanges.

## Is Dalglish Worth Getting?

For players deciding between resources and the 117 OVR Dalglish, the answer depends heavily on their squad.

A 117 OVR striker can still be useful for developing or upgrading a team, but the value of the card depends on its attributes, PlayStyles and how it fits your formation.

If your squad already has significantly higher-rated attackers, taking resources may be more valuable.

In other words, don't automatically choose the highest-rated-looking reward. **Compare the actual value to your squad first.**

## Numero Week 1 Schedule

The Numero chapters are released weekly.

The current schedule listed for the event is:

- **Week 1:** Number 7 — August 27
- **Week 2:** Number 4 — September 3
- **Week 3:** Number 10 — September 10
- **Week 4:** Number 8 — September 17

This means Week 1 is specifically built around the number 7 before the event moves to Number 4 in Week 2.

## Final Verdict

Numero Week 1 is a relatively straightforward event, but it rewards players who consistently return and complete the daily trivia.

The biggest attraction is the combination of **Gems, Star Shards, Draft Vouchers, Rank Up Points and the 117 OVR Dalglish**. Players should decide whether to save toward Dalglish or prioritize resources based on their current squad.

The most important thing is not to overcomplicate it: **complete the daily trivia, collect your tokens and claim the rewards that provide the most value for your team.**
