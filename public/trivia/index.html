About A Nation's Story trivia

A Nation's Story is a recurring FC Mobile event that turns each country's football history into a set of trivia questions. This page collects the France, Mexico, and Brazil chapters in one place, with every question, hint, and answer laid out by day so you can play through them or just check what you missed.

Each nation is split into difficulty levels. France and Mexico run nine days across Beginner, Medium, and Difficult, with three questions per day at Beginner rising to five at Difficult. Brazil's current chapter runs three days at Beginner level, three questions per day. Getting a question wrong still shows you the correct answer and a short explanation, so you can keep learning even if you're not trying to get a perfect score.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are in FC Mobile A Nation's Story trivia?

France and Mexico each have 36 questions across 9 days. Brazil currently has 9 questions across 3 days.

How does the difficulty work?

Each nation is split into Beginner, Medium, and Difficult levels. Every level covers three days of questions, and the number of questions per day increases as the difficulty goes up.

Can I see the answers without playing?

Yes. Every question has a Show Answer option that reveals the correct answer and an explanation, so this page also works as a plain answer guide if you'd rather not quiz yourself.
