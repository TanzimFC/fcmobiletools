Keep this off until Google approves the site. While it is off, no Google script loads and your own ads fill every slot.
Use AdSense when approved

AdSense is separate from house and sponsor promotions. It needs approval, a configured unit, sufficient content, a prior page view and the visitor’s optional ad choice where required.

How often the network goes first

At 100 percent, AdSense gets every slot it has a unit for, and your own ads only fill gaps when it has nothing. Lower it if you want your promos (alerts, giveaways) to keep growing your subscribers.

Ad unit IDs

Create one display ad unit per slot in AdSense (Ads, By ad unit, Display ads), then paste each numeric ID here. A slot with no ID never shows AdSense.
